At **WritOn**, we respect your right to privacy and data autonomy. Users can permanently delete their account and all associated personal data at any time.

---

## 1. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

### Method A: In-App Deletion (Immediate)
1. Open the **WritOn** app on your Android device.
2. Navigate to your **Profile** (bottom right icon) and tap **Settings** (gear icon / 3-dots).
3. Under the **ACCOUNT** section, tap **Delete Account & Data**.
4. Confirm the warning dialog. Your account, profile, and authored content will be removed immediately from our active database.

### Method B: Web / Email Request (If App is Uninstalled)
If you no longer have access to the app or have uninstalled it:
1. Send an email to **saurabh.682@gmail.com** with the subject: `Account Deletion Request - WritOn`.
2. Include the registered email address or pen name associated with your account.
3. Our team will verify and permanently delete your account within **48 to 72 hours**.

---

## 2. What Types of Data Are Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged:
- **Profile Data**: Full name, pen name, email address, profile avatar photo, bio, and location.
- **Authored Stories & Articles**: All published stories, reading notes, and saved drafts.
- **Social & Engagement Records**: All comments, reflections, story applause/likes, and saved bookmarks.
- **Device Identifiers**: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens and session tokens.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Exceptions

- **No Personal Data Retained**: Once deletion is processed, no personal, profile, or content data is retained.
- **Security & Fraud Logs**: Minimal server access logs containing non-personally identifiable network logs may be retained for up to 30 days solely for security monitoring, abuse prevention, and legal compliance, after which they are automatically purged.
