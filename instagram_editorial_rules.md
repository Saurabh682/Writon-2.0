# WritOn Instagram Editorial Rules (`instagram_editorial_rules.md`)

> **Subsystem**: WritOn Global Editorial Subsystem  
> **Brand & Craft Standard**: Warm Parchment Watercolor Aesthetic • Anti-Mannered Direct Statement • 0:00 Proposition Cut Hook

---

## 1. Visual & Graphic Design Philosophy

Instagram is a visual-first medium, but WritOn rejects generic social media aesthetics (neon gradients, 3D device frames, or crowded text). Every asset adheres to the **Warm Parchment Watercolor Aesthetic**:

1. **Canvas & Palette**:
   - Base background: Warm Ivory Parchment (`#FAF5EE`) with delicate fibrous paper texture.
   - Accents: Terracotta blooms (`#D45226` / `#BA431C`) with organic water-bleeding edges.
   - Text color: Classical Ink (`#1C1917`), Muted Ink (`#6E655B`). Strict **WCAG 2.1 AA** contrast ($\ge 4.5:1$).
   - Strictly no obsidian dark mode or stark artificial shadows.
2. **Space & Density**:
   - Every slide must maintain at least **$50\%$ negative space**. 
   - Never crowd margins. Maintain minimum $60\text{px}$ safe margins around all text blocks.
3. **Typography**:
   - Classical Book Serif: `Newsreader` (English) or `Noto Serif Devanagari` (Hindi).
   - Clean UI labels: `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

## 2. Format & Editorial Pacing Architectures

### 2.1 The 10-Slide Carousel Narrative Arc
A carousel is not ten disconnected quotes; it is a unified narrative engine:
- **Slide 1 (`hook`)**: Unfinished business, cognitive friction, or sharp craft tension. No generic titles.
- **Slide 2 (`tension`)**: The friction or dilemma in human writing or daily life.
- **Slide 3 (`proof`)**: Concrete sensory evidence, contrast, or tactile example.
- **Slide 4 (`consequence`)**: What happens when the craft rule is applied vs ignored.
- **Final Slide (`cta`)**: Quiet, low-pressure resolution. Single app destination (`writon.cc/go/...`).

### 2.2 The 0:00 Proposition Cut Hook
- **Feed Image**: Immediate visual tension in the primary typography.
- **Carousel**: Slide 1 headline must deliver a complete, compelling thought within 12 words.
- **Reel Video**: First frame readable; spoken/written proposition occurs within 0:00–0:02. No greeting or logos.
- **Story**: Immediate visual and thematic clarity; no decorative preamble.

### 2.3 Caption Craft & Fold Restraint
- **The 125-Character Fold**: The primary proposition must be readable above the fold before Instagram's `... more` cutoff.
- **Anti-Mannered Direct Prose**: Speak directly. Avoid decorative figurative language ("a dial worth turning" $\to$ "a parameter worth varying").
- **Hashtag Standards**:
  - Always lowercase (e.g. `#writon #writingcommunity #storytelling #books`).
  - Density: 5–8 focused topical and craft discovery tags.

## 3. Governance Gates & Channel Standards

Every Instagram draft must satisfy the 17 Instagram Channel Gates:
- `IG01_CAPTION_LIMIT`: Caption $\le 2200$ chars, core proposition in first 125 chars.
- `IG02_EMPTY_HOOK`: Active curiosity/tension on Slide 1; zero generic title cards.
- `IG03_VISUAL_TEXT_OVERLOAD`: $\ge 50\%$ negative space preserved.
- `IG04_SLIDE_REPETITION`: No adjacent repetitive slide bodies.
- `IG05_CAROUSEL_WITHOUT_PROGRESSION`: Requires `Hook → Tension → Proof → Resolution`.
- `IG06_GENERIC_QUOTE_CARD`: Verifiable human/literary citations only.
- `IG07_UNSUPPORTED_VISUAL_CLAIM`: Provenance bundle must substantiate all claims.
- `IG08_CTA_REPETITION`: Exactly 1 soft CTA across the asset sequence.
- `IG09_MULTIDIMENSIONAL_REPETITION`: Multi-vector comparison (insight, archetype, template, CTA).
- `IG10_SEMANTIC_DUPLICATE`: Cosine similarity $< 0.84$ against recent 30-day publications.
- `IG11_ASPECT_RATIO_STANDARDS`: Format meets design standards (1:1, 4:5, 9:16).
- `IG12_MEDIA_CAPABILITY_MATRIX`: Conforms to Meta v26.0 protocol constraints.
- `IG13_ASSET_UNAVAILABLE`: Pre-signed fetch URLs valid $\ge 30\text{ min}$ with verified SHA-256.
- `IG14_GOVERNANCE_BUNDLE_MISMATCH`: Verifies `governance_bundle_hash` freshness.
- `IG15_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`: Hard facts require primary sources.
- `IG16_ABSTRACT_UNGROUNDED`: Abstract claims must include physical sensory anchors.
- `IG17_ACCESSIBILITY_COMPLIANCE`: Contrast $\ge 4.5:1$, font size $\ge 16\text{pt}$, safe area compliant.
