Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

At WritOn (developed by iBitValley), we maintain a strict, non-negotiable zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Zero Tolerance: Any content, imagery, writing, or communication involving the exploitation, abuse, grooming, or endangerment of minors is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate termination and legal reporting.

1. Prohibited Content & Behavior

WritOn strictly forbids:

  • Any visual, written, or implied depiction of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE) in any form.
  • Any attempt to groom, solicit, endanger, or inappropriately interact with minors.
  • Content promoting, facilitating, or encouraging harm towards children.

2. In-App Reporting & Content Moderation

We provide immediate mechanisms for users to flag and report any safety concerns:

  • In-App Reporting: Users can report any story, comment, or user profile directly by tapping the more options (3 dots) menu on any post or author profile and selecting Report.
  • Rapid Takedown: Reported content is prioritized, reviewed by our moderation team, and removed immediately upon confirmation.
  • Account Termination: Accounts that violate child safety standards are banned permanently and prohibited from creating new accounts.

3. Reporting to Law Enforcement & Authorities

WritOn complies fully with all applicable international, national, and regional child safety laws. When CSAM or CSAE is identified:

  • We immediately preserve all relevant evidentiary data.
  • We file immediate reports with the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant regional and national law enforcement agencies.
  • We cooperate transparently and proactively with legal authorities to aid in child protection investigations.
