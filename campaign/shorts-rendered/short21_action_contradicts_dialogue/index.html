The Scene
Her hand wipes an already-clean counter.
Would you believe her?
Spoken Words
“I’m fine,” she said.
Contradicting Action
“I’m fine.” She wiped the counter again.
Subtext in Action
“Take your time,” he said—
checking his watch for the third time.
THE ACTION CAN DISAGREE.
LET ACTION DISAGREE.
Characters lie. Movement confesses.
