WRITING HACK #12
WritOn.
Make her terrifying.
without raising her voice
She was furious at him for lying.
Delete the emotion.
Lethal Restraint
She refolded his napkin
into a sharp triangle,
and slid the salt cellar
two inches left.
Loud anger makes noise.
Lethal anger organizes the room.
