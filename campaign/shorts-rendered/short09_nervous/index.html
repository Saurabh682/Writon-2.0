WRITING HACK #09
WritOn.
Broken Draft Too Abstract
“She was extremely nervous about the interview.”
Healed Sentence Physical Tell
She peeled the cardboard sleeve
off her paper coffee cup,
then began tearing it into
clean, narrow strips.
Now the reader is in the waiting room with her.
Give the body a physical task.
The emotion takes care of itself.
