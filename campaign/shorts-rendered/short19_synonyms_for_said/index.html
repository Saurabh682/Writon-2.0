Writing Hack #19
WritOn
STOP HUNTING SYNONYMS FOR “SAID”.
replace tags with action
Decorative Tag
“I’m fine,” she exclaimed defensively.
The Action Beat
“I’m fine.” She wiped the counter again.
Adverb Crutch
“Wait,” he whispered nervously.
The Physical Beat
“Wait.” His hand gripped the keys in his pocket.
“SAID” DISAPPEARS.
A beat of action stays.
