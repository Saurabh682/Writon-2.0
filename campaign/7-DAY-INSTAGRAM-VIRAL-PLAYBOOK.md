# WritOn 7-Day Instagram Viral Growth Playbook (Week 1)

This playbook is designed to scale WritOn from 0 followers using high-retention 5–7 second aesthetic B-roll Reels paired with high-converting Carousel and Story link stickers.

---

## 🎯 The Core Viral Mechanics

1. **The 5–7 Second Loop Trick**:
   - Keep video length strictly **5 to 7 seconds**.
   - When viewers read your on-screen text, the video loops 2–3 times automatically.
   - Instagram’s algorithm registers a **200%–300% completion rate**, triggering instant distribution to non-followers on Explore.

2. **Zero-Face Aesthetic B-Roll**:
   - You don't need a camera crew or to show your face.
   - Film simple 5-second clips on your phone:
     - Steaming chai / coffee mug on a desk
     - Evening sky / sunset through trees
     - Rain on a car or bedroom window
     - Hands typing on a laptop or writing in a notebook
     - Night city lights with soft blur (bokeh)

3. **Audio Selection**:
   - Always select audio with the **↗ (Trending Arrow)** in Instagram Reels.
   - Pick calm, acoustic, lofi, piano, or slowed+reverb tracks that match a literary mood.

## ⚡ Daily 3-Minute Routine:
1. **Record a 5s clip** while going about your day (sky, tea, desk, book).
2. **Open Reels**, select the clip.
3. **Type the on-screen text hook** using a clean serif or typewriter font.
4. **Select a Trending Audio** with the ↗ icon.
5. **Paste the caption** and tap **Share**!
