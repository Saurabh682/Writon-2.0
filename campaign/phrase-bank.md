## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
Calm, literary, confident but not loud. We are a quiet space for focus, not a noisy social feed.

### Anti-Mannered Prose (Direct Statement Rule)
> *"Mannered prose substitutes metaphor and flourish for direct statement... The phrases exist to display the writer, not to convey the idea, and readers can tell. That is why mannered prose irritates: it makes the reader work harder so the writer can perform. It is also imprecise. Metaphors drag in connotations the writer did not choose and cannot control. The fix is to say what you mean. When a literal phrase is available, use it."*

**Rules for WritOn Copy, Cards & Captions:**
1. **Convey the Idea, Don't Perform**: Never use ornate metaphors to show off writerly flair (e.g. avoid *"turning dials"*, *"earning its keep"*, *"weaving tapestries of soul"*). State the observation or exercise directly.
2. **Prefer Literal Precision**: If a literal, direct description exists, use it instead of an ornamental substitution.
3. **No Unearned Tropes or Poetic Bloat**: Avoid pretentious adjectives, decorative aphorisms, or strained figurative language. Let the clarity of the craft tip or prompt carry the weight.
4. **Respect Reader Energy**: Do not make the reader decode layered figurative embellishments just to understand a 2-line writing exercise or prompt.

### Forbidden Phrases & Patterns
- "Smash that like button"
- "Link in bio!!!" (Keep it to standard punctuation: "Link in bio.")
- "You won't believe what happens next"
- "Hurry up and download"
- "Viral"
- Decorative writer-performance idioms (*"unleash your inner muse"*, *"paint with words"*, *"words take flight"*, *"dive into the symphony of thought"*)
