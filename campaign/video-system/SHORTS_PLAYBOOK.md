# WritOn Shorts Editorial System & Production Constitution

## 0. Master Editorial Brain Authority (`campaign/EDITORIAL_BRAIN.json`)
**Strict Invariant**: Every post, hook, script, and visual transformation decision MUST originate from and pass through [`campaign/EDITORIAL_BRAIN.json`](file:///d:/VibeCode/WritOn-PowerUp/campaign/EDITORIAL_BRAIN.json).
- **Zero AI Slop Hard Gates**: All candidate concepts and scripts must pass the 29 blockers before reaching the brief stage.
- **Cadence & Proposition Spacing**: Enforce a minimum 48-hour separation between identical proposition archetypes.
- **Sensory Anchors Over Abstractions**: Ground every emotional scene in physical objects and tactile tells.
- **Anti-Repetition Audit**: Every proposed topic must clear both the Brain's historical ledger and `CONTENT_MEMORY.json`.

## 1. Audience
Writers actively trying to improve fiction, essays, literary prose, poetry, and narrative craft. Serious, thoughtful readers who value slow prose and aesthetic depth.

## 2. Primary Series Franchise
**WRITING HACK**
- Tagline: *Fix your writing one sentence at a time.*
- The metaphor: A miniature sentence surgery / tiny literary repair shop.

## 3. Core Promise
Fix exactly **one** writing problem in under 30 seconds.

## 4. Standard Surgical Structure (20–26s Sweet Spot)
- **0.0s – 2.0s (Frame Zero)**:
  Show the flawed sentence immediately on the desk. No logos, no intro animations, no verbal preambles.
- **2.0s – 6.0s (The Diagnosis)**:
  State precisely why it fails (*"This sentence tells me she's nervous. It doesn't make me feel it."*).
- **6.0s – 9.0s (The Gentle Dissolve)**:
  The weak words gently dim and dissolve into the warm parchment (no harsh strikethrough lines).
- **9.0s – 18.0s (The Rewrite)**:
  The healed sentence builds line-by-line synchronized with the voice.
- **18.0s – 22.0s (The Contrast / Consequence)**:
  Show what changed (*"Now the reader is sitting in the room with her."*).
- **22.0s – 26.0s (The Craft Rule & Semantic Loop)**:
  Deliver one memorable craft maxim that loops semantically back into the bad sentence on Frame Zero.

## 5. Ideal Video Invariant
- One craft problem.
- One sentence.
- One transformation.
- One takeaway.

## 6. The 8-Stage Operating Workflow
1. **Select**: Choose 1 craft problem from the taxonomy in `IDEA_BACKLOG.json`.
2. **Research Memory**: Audit `CONTENT_MEMORY.json` against the 10-video rolling window.
3. **Brief**: Complete `brief.json` with explicit `desired_learning`.
4. **Hook Tournament**: Generate 8 candidates, score against 5 tests, select the winner.
5. **Draft**: Produce 20-26s script (Bad -> Why it fails -> Principle -> Better -> Rule).
6. **Critic Pass**: Score Craft Value (>=8/10) and Video Value (>=8/10). Apply 10 rejection blockers.
7. **Visualize**: Generate timed `shotlist.json` and 1080x1920 `render_config.json`.
8. **Render & Learn**: Synthesize audio, capture previews, render, publish, and record postmortem.

## 7. Brutal Rejection Pass (Hard Gates)
A script is REJECTED before rendering if ANY of these hold true:
- [ ] Viewer cannot identify the problem by second 2.
- [ ] Opening requires voiceover to be understood (Frame 0 must be self-explanatory).
- [ ] Weak sentence is unrealistically bad or hyperbolic.
- [ ] Rewrite is only prettier, not structurally or tactically better.
- [ ] Lesson requires more than one concept.
- [ ] Payoff merely repeats the opening hook without giving a usable rule.
- [ ] Wording sounds like generic AI motivational advice.
- [ ] Core object, emotion, or scene resembles one used in the previous 10 videos.
- [ ] Final line exists only as an artificial loop trick rather than a true craft insight.
- [ ] The video would work equally well as a static quote card (transformation must be essential).

## 8. Two-Gate Quality Threshold
- **Craft Value (>= 8/10)**: Would a published fiction writer or essayist find this advice legitimate and useful?
- **Video Value (>= 8/10)**: Does the visual and vocal tension compel the viewer to watch through to the rewrite?
