# Hook Tournament: Short #10 ("Stop Writing 'He Heard' and 'She Noticed'")

## 1. Candidate Generation
1. *Stop putting a filter between your character and the noise.*
2. *“He heard” is stealing all the urgency from this scene.*
3. *Delete these two words and watch the sentence jump.*
4. *This sentence doesn't have an emergency. It has a reporter.*
5. *Stop telling me what your character is hearing.*
6. *Your reader doesn't need to be told the ears are working.*
7. *Kill the filter word. Let the footsteps do the work.*
8. *Every time you write “he heard”, the danger moves one step further away.*
