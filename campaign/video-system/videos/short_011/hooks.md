# Hook Tournament: Short #11 (Dialogue Tags / Adverbs)
Target: Kill adverb dialogue tags with tactile action beats.

### Candidate 1 (Contrarian Statement)
"Your dialogue tag is apologizing for your sentence."
- Strength: Punchy, psychological insight, makes the writer re-evaluate immediately.
- Score: 9.4/10

### Candidate 2 (Direct Diagnosis)
"Adverbs in dialogue tags are a confession that your words aren't working."
- Strength: Accurate, but a bit syllable-heavy for the 0:00-0:02 cut window.
- Score: 8.7/10

### Candidate 3 (Provocative Rule)
"Never tell the reader how to hear a line."
- Strength: Strong philosophy, clean phrasing.
- Score: 8.9/10

### Candidate 4 (The Inversion)
"If your character is dangerous, stop writing 'he whispered menacingly'."
- Strength: Cites the exact phrase, very relatable.
- Score: 9.1/10

### Candidate 5 (Tactile Confrontation)
"This dialogue tag doesn't add tension. It begs for it."
- Strength: Razor-sharp parallel to Short #10's ("This sentence doesn't have an emergency. It has a reporter.").
- Score: 9.5/10

### Candidate 6 (Craft Authority)
"A strong line of dialogue doesn't need an adverb chaperone."
- Strength: Witty, memorable metaphor.
- Score: 9.0/10

### Candidate 7 (Action vs Label)
"Menacing is an action, not an adverb."
- Strength: Crisp, under 8 words.
- Score: 8.8/10

### Candidate 8 (Surgical Cut)
"Delete the adverb. Let the character move."
- Strength: Actionable imperative.
- Score: 9.0/10

---

## Tournament Winner
**Candidate 5 + Candidate 1 Fusion**:
> **Spoken Hook (0.0s - 2.2s)**:  
> *“This dialogue tag doesn't add tension. It's apologizing for it.”*  
> **Turn (2.2s - 5.5s)**:  
> *“If the line is dangerous, make his hands prove it.”*
