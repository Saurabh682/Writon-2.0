# Hook Tournament: Short #12 (How to Write Anger Without Screaming)
Target: Replace soap-opera shouting with domestic lethal composure.

### Candidate 1 (Tactile Confrontation)
"This sentence says furious. It sounds like a soap opera."
- Strength: Direct diagnosis of the viewer's draft, parallels our best performers.
- Score: 9.3/10

### Candidate 2 (The Contrarian Rule)
"Never let an angry character raise their voice."
- Strength: Pure counter-consensus, provokes immediate reaction.
- Score: 9.5/10

### Candidate 3 (The Observation)
"Loud anger is noisy. Lethal anger is organized."
- Strength: Elegant, memorable, great craft truth.
- Score: 9.2/10

### Candidate 4 (The Inversion)
"If she is truly furious, she won't slam the door."
- Strength: Concrete negative constraint.
- Score: 9.0/10

### Candidate 5 (The Diagnosis)
"Telling us she was furious lets the character off the hook."
- Strength: Good writerly rationale, but slightly academic for 0:00.
- Score: 8.6/10

### Candidate 6 (The Behavioral Shift)
"When humans are terrified, they freeze. When they are murderous, they get polite."
- Strength: Fascinating psychological insight.
- Score: 9.1/10

### Candidate 7 (The Frame 0 Cut)
"This sentence tells us she is angry. It forgets to make her terrifying."
- Strength: Sharp parallel to Short #09.
- Score: 9.4/10

### Candidate 8 (Surgical Maxim)
"Stop writing anger with exclamation marks."
- Strength: Punchy, but focuses on punctuation rather than character prose.
- Score: 8.8/10
