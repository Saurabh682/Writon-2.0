# WritOn Video Brand & Aesthetic Rules

## 1. Visual Standards (The Tabletop Room)
- **Canvas**: 1080 × 1920 portrait (standard YouTube Shorts delivery). 30 FPS.
- **Background**: Authentic high-resolution overhead desk photography (`desk_coffee_card_nobox_4k.jpg`), warm ceramic coffee cup, fountain pens, natural directional daylight.
- **Card**: Warm Ivory Parchment (`#FAF5EE`), zero nested box clutter, zero heavy borders.
- **Typography**: 
  - Headlines & Quotes: `Playfair Display` (Classic book serif, high contrast, true italics).
  - Badges & UI Pills: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean geometric sans-serif, bold, tracked).
- **Accents**: 
  - Left margin rule: Crimson (`#821D1A`), 3px width.
  - Inversion Stamp: `#7D1B17` background with white uppercase text (`WRITING HACK #XX`).
  - Diagnostic Tag: Soft blush `#F8EBEA` with crimson outline `#D66D67`.
  - Rewrite Highlight: Pure italic crimson `#821D1A`.
- **Transitions**: Gentle typographic fade-out and slide (`opacity: 0, y: -25, duration: 1.2s`). Never use animated strikethrough SVG lines.

## 2. Kinetic Timing & Velocity Architecture (Zero-Plateau Gate)
- **Max Duration**: 18.0s – 20.0s target window.
- **The 1.5-Second Visual Progression Invariant**: Text or UI states must never remain static without micro-movement, highlight, badge entry, or line reveal for more than 1.5 seconds at any point in the video.
- **High-Velocity Timeline Contract**:
  - `0.0s – 0.8s`: Interactive challenge hook (`MAKE THIS LINE FEEL DANGEROUS.` / *without changing the dialogue*) + frame 0 tactile audio snap.
  - `0.8s – 1.8s`: Flawed sentence clean reveal.
  - `1.8s – 2.5s`: Marker highlight snaps onto offending word/modifier.
  - `2.5s – 3.5s`: Micro-directive badge enters (`DELETE ONE WORD.`) + flawed word dissolves.
  - `3.5s – 7.0s`: Staggered line reveal of healed action rewrite (accompanied only by contextual badge e.g. `Physical Threat`; never clinical labels).
  - `7.0s – 11.0s`: Craft consequence sentence.
  - `11.0s – 17.0s`: Closing maxim & high-contrast rule.
  - `17.0s – 18.0s`: Hold / loop reset. Voiceover must not exceed 16–17s.

## 3. Acoustic Architecture
- **Voiceover**: Nicole (`af_nicole` @ 1.20–1.25x speed) via Kokoro-82M ONNX. Crisp, natural cadence.
- **Tactile Foley**: Crisp mechanical / physical sound cue at frame 0.0s (deadbolt latch, typewriter strike, page snap) and on key transformation moments.
- **Musical Bed**: `official_writon_piano.mp3` layered at low volume (`volume=0.038`), smooth fade-out.

## 4. Title & Packaging Rules
- **Title**: Chases viewer search intent directly (e.g. `How to Write Nervousness Without Saying "Nervous"`).
  - Do NOT put "Writing Hack:" in the title (the franchise stamp is inside the video).
  - Do NOT append `#shorts` in the title.
  - Length: Under 50 characters (mobile screen safe).
- **Description**: 
  - First 1–2 lines: Searchable craft summary defining the fiction technique.
  - Middle: Narrative contrast and concrete rewrite example.
  - Links: Official vanity redirects (`https://writon.cc`, `https://writon.cc/playstore`, `https://writon.cc/instagram`, `https://writon.cc/x`, `https://writon.cc/threads`).
  - Tags: Lowercase only, 6–10 high-intent tags.
