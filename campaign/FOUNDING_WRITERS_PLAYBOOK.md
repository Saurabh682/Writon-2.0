## 1. The Concrete Trade We Are Offering (Strictly Deliverable)

We do **NOT** promise badges, weekly stats, or spotlight priority algorithms before they exist. We offer three bounded, authentic promises:

1. **A Real Reader at the Other End**: The founder (`Saurabh Kumar`) personally reads your first few pieces and writes a thoughtful, personal response.
2. **A Direct Voice in the Product**: Tell me what feels wrong, missing, or unnecessary in the editor and reader. You have a direct line to what gets built next.
3. **Founding Writer Status**: Recognition as one of the first 25 writers shaping WritOn from the beginning.
4. **Multilingual & Pen Names Welcome**: Write in English, हिन्दी, मराठी, or বাংলা. Pen names are fully supported.

**What we ask in return**: Publish 2 or 3 pieces this month, and give honest, unfiltered feedback on what feels off.

### Post 3: Honest Audit — What 30 posts taught me
- **Scheduled**: Monday, Sep 28, 2026 (9:00 AM IST)
- **Format**: `TEXT_ONLY` (Plain text test to contrast against image posts)
- **Operational Requirement**: Cross-check numbers with live LinkedIn Analytics before dispatch.
- **Post Copy**:
```text
I posted about 30 times in two weeks and got roughly 1,200 impressions in total. I have 23 followers. Almost nobody commented.

Here's what I learned:

1. My best posts were the ones where I said something I actually believed. A post that was only a link got 3 impressions.
2. Posting more didn't help. Some days I posted four or five times, and the posts competed with each other.
3. Many of my captions opened with a label instead of an idea.

So I'm changing three things. One post a day at most. One idea per post. And the time I save goes into talking to writers directly.

If you're building something small: what did you change after your first two weeks?
```
- **First Comment**: None.
