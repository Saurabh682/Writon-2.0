At **WritOn**, we maintain a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

---

## 1. Prohibited Actions & Materials
WritOn strictly prohibits:
- Any creation, publishing, upload, or distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
- Any depiction of Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
- Any attempt to groom, solicit, endanger, or inappropriately interact with minors.

---

## 2. In-App Reporting Mechanism
- Users can flag any content or user profile using the in-app **Report** action on any story, reflection/comment, or profile.
- Critical safety reports are routed with highest priority to the safety team and removed immediately upon review.

---

## 3. Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC
- We comply with all applicable child safety laws.
- When CSAM or CSAE is identified, content is removed immediately, account is permanently terminated, and reports are filed with the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** and relevant law enforcement authorities.
