Account & Data Deletion

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

At WritOn, we believe you should have complete ownership and control over your personal data and creative publications.

How to Delete Your Account from Inside the App:

  • Open the WritOn app on your Android device.
  • Go to your Profile tab (bottom right).
  • Tap the Settings icon (top right gear).
  • Scroll to the bottom and select "Delete Account".
  • Confirm your choice. Your account, profile, comments, applauds, and published stories will be permanently deleted immediately.

Request Deletion via Web / Email:

If you cannot access the app, you can request manual deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us:

Email: help@writon.co
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Please include the email address associated with your WritOn account. Deletion will be processed within 48 hours.

Data That is Deleted:

  • Your user profile, bio, and display name
  • All published stories, poems, and drafts
  • Comments and applauds given or received
  • Reading history and saved bookmarks
