Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

WritOn is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and family-appropriate creative writing and reading environment for all users.

Zero Tolerance for Harmful Content:

WritOn maintains strict, zero-tolerance policies against:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Predatory behavior, grooming, harassment, or bullying targeting minors.
  • Graphic violence, self-harm promotion, or dangerous illicit material.

Content Moderation & Reporting:

Every story and comment on WritOn includes an in-app Report Content option. Our moderation team and automated content safety checks review reported material promptly.

Urgent Reporting: If you discover any content violating child safety standards, report it via the app or email us directly at help@writon.cc for immediate investigation and removal.
