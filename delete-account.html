Account & Data Deletion Request

At WritOn (developed by iBitValley), we value and respect your privacy. You have full control over your personal account and any data you create on our platform.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Option 1: In the Android App (Instant)
  1. Open the WritOn app on your Android device.
  2. Go to your Profile tab (bottom right) and tap the Settings icon.
  3. Under ACCOUNT, tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm your decision. Your account, profile, and authored content will be removed immediately.
Option 2: Submit an Email / Web Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, send an email to our support team:

Email: saurabh.682@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request - WritOn
Include: The email address or username associated with your WritOn account.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon receiving your deletion request, the following data is permanently purged from our servers:

  • User Profile: Full name, username/pen name, email address, bio, profile avatar photo, and location.
  • Authored Content: All stories, articles, notes, and drafts created under your account.
  • Engagement Data: All comments, replies, applause reactions, and bookmarks.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging push notification tokens.

Data Retention & Processing Period

  • Processing Time: In-app deletions are executed immediately. Email requests are verified and permanently purged within 48 to 72 hours.
  • Data Retention: No personal or identifiable user data is retained after deletion. Standard anonymized server security logs are retained strictly for fraud prevention for up to 30 days, after which they are automatically expunged.
